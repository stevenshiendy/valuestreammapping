● Lean / Toyota Production System

One tonne of steel.
26 days to travel 420 metres.

This is the story of a single floor's worth of rebar on a 12-storey building in Bandung. You will follow it, map it, find where its time goes, then fix the flow yourself, one lean lever at a time.

Companion to the article "Value Stream Mapping for Material Flow Optimization in Construction Projects" by Steven Glenn Shiendy, Global Supply Chain Management (LA76), BINUS University Bandung. Case figures are simulated for teaching and calibrated from published open-access studies.

    PCE = VA hours / (LT × 10)
